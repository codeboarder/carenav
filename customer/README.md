## Contoso Health Context

CareNav Florida was developed with direct insight from managing care transitions at large health systems.

The platform addresses real-world challenges faced by discharge planners and case managers who handle thousands of elder care transitions annually, navigating complex Medicaid eligibility rules, VA benefits, and facility placement decisions.

For health systems:

- **Improved Placement Rates**: Better facility matching increases first-choice placements
- **Compliance Assurance**: Validated eligibility calculations reduce denial rates
- **Staff Efficiency**: AI assistance reduces research time from 20+ hours to minutes
